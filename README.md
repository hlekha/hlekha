Hello, pleased to have you here at my repo! 

## A Little About Me:
I am an undergraduate student studying Financial Mathematics at Baruch College. Long story short, I've always loved STEM and wanted to become an aerospace engineer, almost got the chance to meet Jim Simons before knowing who he was, I then found out who he was and became inspired by his story of revolutionizing finance with mathematics. Hence, I now aspire to be a worker in the quantitative finance field (all roles within this field interest me so I am open to all), and hope that this portfolio accurately showcases my passion for STEM and applying it to solve real puzzles.

## What I'm Working On
I'm currently working on my Quantum Mechanics repository. This is for my Special Topics in Theoretical Physics class, where I am self-studying Quantum Mechanics. In this repository I code certain problems, and concepts seen in the book. To see more on this journey you can go to.

I'm also working on an algorithm to solve the October 2026 Jane Street puzzle, and I plan to generalize this puzzle so that the algorithm works for any n x n grid and not just Jane Street's 11 x 11 puzzle with its given clues.

Finally, I am also a project member in the Financial Quants & Engineers club. Here I am working on a project which prices options using a reinforcement learning model that implements a variety of famous option pricing models.
